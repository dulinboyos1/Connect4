# Connect 4

A simple Connect 4 game implemented in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Play the Game

You can play the game online at: [https://joemw.github.io/Connect4/](https://joemw.github.io/Connect4/)

## How to Play

- Click on a column to drop your piece.
- The first player to get four pieces in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) wins.

## Local Development

Open `index.html` in your web browser to play locally.
